Perler Beads FAQ

Perler beads are small, plastic fusible beads. You arrange them on a pegboard, then iron them to melt together into a solid design.

Use a medium heat (without steam). Iron in small circular motions for about 10–20 seconds until beads fuse but holes remain visible.

Yes, pegboards are reusable. Wash them gently with warm water and soap if wax from ironing builds up.
